# Reference audit — 9 September 2026

51 unique external URLs in the walkthrough and source registry were individually clicked from an index of their exact URLs. Destination pages and downloaded PDF text were checked against the associated claims. PDF links were also clicked in Chrome; some sources download instead of displaying inline, and some lack accessible PDF text.

Changes: replaced inaccessible Conifer, ZF, Turntide and Renault links; linked Ola to its exact certification filing and Audi to its technical article; corrected source titles and dates; added primary DOE, CLEPA and ACMA evidence. Removed unsupported universal magnet percentages, temperature thresholds, price and licence-denial assertions. Distinguished prototypes, certification and production, corrected Renault timing and Ather chemistry wording, and separated Proterial maximum output from maximum speed.
