Teaching simplifications & evidence limits
Field lobes are a few clean lines, not a computed field.
Field length is relative within this chapter only and carries no unit.
Four large grains stand in for a real microstructure.
The reversed patch is drawn at one corner for legibility; real loss is not corner-shaped.
Reversal is shown as a boundary being crossed, not as a measured threshold.
Protection is drawn as flecks and shells, never as a composition or a fraction.
Heat is a relative aura with no temperature, slope or grade attached.
Cooling is drawn as one loop reaching the rotor region; no flow rate or capability is implied.
SmCo appears as a position, not a comparison, with no limit, price or parity claim.
