Mechanical assumptions
Brand-neutral hatchback silhouette. Not modelled on any production vehicle.
Single rear drive unit by default. The two-motor toggle adds a front unit of the same geometry; it is presented as an option, not a norm.
Battery drawn as one floor-mounted pack between the axles. Module count and cell format are illustrative.
Motor, single-speed reduction gear, differential and inverter drawn as one bolted unit. Separate-inverter layouts also exist and are not shown.
Suspension shown as a generic spring, damper and lower arm. Not a specific geometry.
One liquid cooling loop serving pack, inverter and motor. Loop routing is schematic.
Gear pair drawn as two meshing circles. Ratio, tooth count and stage count are not asserted.
No dimension, mass, power, torque, voltage or speed figure appears anywhere in this sequence.
Coolant colour #5E8F86 is added to the notation key here and is reserved for coolant only.
Assets export as flat SVG layer groups with the ids above, no text elements inside. All type in these frames is DOM, positioned from the anchor table, and remains editable.
